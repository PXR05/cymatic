The shared/peq module vendors Contour's Kotlin device protocols, DSP, device
records, catalog generator, and Android HID client. No Contour UI is included.
Modifications: removed unused serialization annotations from Model.kt; omitted
TextCodecs.kt (Cymatic uses its own preset files); adapted Gradle source and record
paths; strengthened session guards for disconnect/cancellation and HID selection.
Cymatic supplies its own device-aware editor, draft storage, permissions, backups
and reconnect/read-back flow. Unverified upstream features stay disabled.

Supplied NOTICE and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md retain upstream attribution, including
devicePEQ (0BSD) and FreeDSP-Studio (MIT). Licence texts are supplied with the app.
